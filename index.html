A paradigm shift

Arcade is the first AI physical product creation platform that lets anyone design, purchase, and sell custom, manufacturable products using natural language and generative AI. We're building the connective tissue that embeds cost, feasibility, aesthetics, and manufacturing intelligence directly into the creative process. We believe the same shift that transformed digital content is about to hit physical commerce. Shopify unlocked independent digital sellers. Canva unlocked design. Figma unlocked collaborative product building. Arcade unlocks AI‑native product creation for physical goods. Backed by world-class investors including Reid Hoffman, Forerunner, Canaan, Elad Gil, and more, Arcade has enabled millions to generate physical product designs of their dreams.

Our values

At Arcade, values are operating principles, not posters. They define the bar for the products we ship, the people we hire, and the kind of culture we're building. If they resonate, you'll likely feel at home here; if they don't, you probably won't — and that's by design.

Speed

We are operating on the bleeding edge of AI and commerce. We move quickly, but we stay intentional with cost, manufacturability, and taste. We'd rather ship, observe, and iterate than debate abstractions for weeks.

Taste

Our work lives at the intersection of engineering and aesthetics. We value people who can argue about model architectures and loss functions in the same breath as product photography, interior design, or jewelry detail. Beautiful systems, not just beautiful screenshots.

Systems

Our different products and surfaces are not separate businesses; they're one platform expressed through multiple entry points. We look for people who think in systems: how decisions in AI research affect supply chain, how marketplace UX affects community growth, how pricing models affect model design.

Our team

Arcade is led by Mariam Naficy, Founder and Executive Chairman of Minted, the marketplace for creative goods she built from scratch into a $300M+ profitable business, and the founder of Eve.com, the world's first beauty ecommerce site, launched in 1998. Mariam has also made early investments in companies including Reddit, Gusto, Our Place, and held board positions at Victoria's Secret, Yelp, Medium, Williams College, Stanford GSB, among others. She is joined by a team with deep experience in AI research, design systems, and supply chain from companies such as OpenAI, Google, Apple, Glean, Sierra, Stitch Fix, Gucci, Saint Laurent, Gap, Stability AI, NVIDIA, Amazon, and LinkedIn.
